# Welcome Unit 简单句扩展训练模板

> 目标：把“会说一个短句”训练成“能围绕同一意思自然增加信息”，为后续 Think Level 1 口语和写作建立句子扩展能力。
>
> 原则：不是追求复杂长难句，而是学习在正确简单句上逐层增加 **时间、地点、人物、频率、方式、原因和结果**。

---

# 1. 通用扩展阶梯

从一个最小正确句开始：

> I play badminton.

按下面顺序逐层增加信息。

## Level 1：加时间

> I play badminton **after school**.

提示词：
- every day
- after school
- on weekends
- in the evening
- on Saturday morning

模板：

> I ______ **[when]**.

---

## Level 2：加人物 / 地点

> I play badminton after school **with my friends**.

或：

> I play badminton after school **at the sports center**.

模板：

> I ______ [when] **with ______ / at ______**.

---

## Level 3：加频率

> I **usually** play badminton after school with my friends.

可替换：
- always
- usually
- often
- sometimes
- hardly ever
- never

模板：

> I **[frequency]** ______ [when] [with whom / where].

注意：频率副词位置也作为语法迁移训练。

---

## Level 4：加原因

> I usually play badminton after school with my friends **because it's fun**.

模板：

> I ______ because ______.

原因库：
- it's fun
- it's interesting
- it's relaxing
- it's good exercise
- I can spend time with my friends
- it helps me feel better

---

## Level 5：加第二个相关信息

> I usually play badminton after school with my friends because it's fun. **We sometimes play for about an hour.**

这一步不要求把所有信息硬塞进一个超长句。

目标是从：

> 一个短句

发展为：

> 一个信息较完整的长句 + 一个自然补充句。

这通常比强行制造复杂句更自然。

# 2. 家长通用提问法：5W1H 扩句

孩子先说：

> I read books.

家长不要直接给扩展后的答案，只依次追问：

- **When?** — When do you read books?
- **Where?** — Where do you read?
- **Who with?** — Do you read alone or with someone?
- **How often?** — How often do you read?
- **Why?** — Why do you like reading?
- **How long?** — How long do you usually read?

孩子逐步形成：

> I usually read books in my room after dinner because reading helps me relax.

或者：

> I usually read books in my room after dinner. I often read for about thirty minutes because it helps me relax.

## 家长原则

一次最多追问 2–3 项，不要求每句话都包含全部 5W1H。

---

# 3. Welcome A：自我介绍扩句

## 基础句

> I'm from Wuhan.

### 扩展 1：加入居住信息

> I'm from Wuhan, and I live there with my family.

### 扩展 2：加入时间

> I'm from Wuhan, and I've lived there with my family for many years.

> 注：如果时态尚未掌握，不要求使用 `I've lived`，可降级为：
>
> I'm from Wuhan, and I live there with my family.

### Welcome 阶段推荐版本

> I'm from Wuhan, and I live there with my parents.

---

## 基础句

> I like English.

逐层扩展：

> I like English **very much**.
>
> I like English very much **because it's interesting**.
>
> I like English very much because it's interesting, **and I want to speak it well**.

训练模板：

> I like ______ because ______, and I want to ______.

---

# 4. Welcome B：教室与物品扩句

## 基础句

> The book is on the desk.

逐层扩展：

> The English book is on the desk.
>
> The English book is on the desk **next to my pencil case**.
>
> The English book is on the desk next to my pencil case **because I'm using it now**.

Welcome 阶段核心做到前两层即可。

模板：

> The ______ is ______ the ______ next to / near / between ______.

---

## 基础句

> I like these books.

扩展：

> I really like these books.
>
> I really like these books **because the stories are interesting**.
>
> I really like these books because the stories are interesting, **so I often read them after school**.

训练重点：同时复习 `these / them`。

---

# 5. Welcome C：My Day 扩句

这是 Welcome Unit 最适合系统做扩句训练的部分。

## 基础句

> I get up at seven.

### 加顺序

> I get up at seven, and then I wash my face.

### 加频率

> I usually get up at seven, and then I wash my face.

### 加下一步

> I usually get up at seven, and then I wash my face and have breakfast.

### 形成连续表达

> I usually get up at seven. Then I wash my face and have breakfast. After that, I go to school.

重点：

不要强求写成一个很长的句子。

要训练孩子理解：

**长表达 ≠ 一个无限加 and 的句子。**

---

## My Day 通用扩展模板

起点：

> I ______.

第一层：

> I ______ at ______.

第二层：

> I usually ______ at ______.

第三层：

> I usually ______ at ______, and then I ______.

第四层：

> I usually ______ at ______, and then I ______ because ______.

第五层：改成自然短段落：

> I usually ______ at ______. Then I ______. After that, I ______. I like / don't like ______ because ______.

# 6. Welcome D：My Things 扩句

## `have got` 基础句

> I've got a bike.

扩展：

> I've got a new bike.
>
> I've got a new bike **at home**.
>
> I've got a new bike at home, **and I often ride it on weekends**.
>
> I've got a new bike at home, and I often ride it on weekends **because it's good exercise**.

推荐最终表达：

> I've got a new bike, and I often ride it on weekends because it's good exercise.

---

## `I'd like` 基础句

> I'd like a sandwich.

扩展：

> I'd like a cheese sandwich.
>
> I'd like a cheese sandwich **for lunch**.
>
> I'd like a cheese sandwich for lunch **and some orange juice, please**.

情景任务：

> You are ordering lunch. Start with one thing and expand your order to three pieces of information.

# 7. 六种常用扩句模块

以后任何 Unit 都可以复用。

## ① 时间 When

> I play basketball **after school**.

## ② 地点 Where

> I play basketball after school **in the school gym**.

## ③ 人物 Who with

> I play basketball after school in the school gym **with my classmates**.

## ④ 频率 How often

> I **often** play basketball after school in the school gym with my classmates.

## ⑤ 原因 Why

> I often play basketball after school with my classmates **because it's exciting**.

## ⑥ 方式 / 时长 How / How long

> I often play basketball after school with my classmates **for about an hour** because it's exciting.

推荐顺序不是固定的，重点是让孩子逐步学会“还能补充什么信息”。

# 8. 口语撤支架训练

## Stage 1：完整框架

> I usually ______ [activity] ______ [when] with ______ [who] because ______ [why].

## Stage 2：提示词

> activity → when → who → why

## Stage 3：只问问题

> What do you do?
> When?
> Who with?
> Why?

## Stage 4：完全无提示

> Tell me about your free time.

要求不是背出原句，而是自己组织信息。

# 9. “一句变长句”快速练习卡

家长每次随便给一个基础句，孩子抽取 2–3 个扩展维度。

基础句：

> I read.

扩展：
- what
- when
- where
- why

参考方向：

---

## Card B

基础句：

> I go to school.

扩展：
- when
- how
- who with

参考方向：

> I usually go to school at seven thirty by car with my dad.

---

## Card C

基础句：

> I like swimming.

扩展：
- how often
- where
- who with
- why

参考方向：

> I often go swimming at the sports center with my dad because it's fun and good exercise.

基础句：

# 10. 家长纠错规则

扩句训练最容易出现的问题是：信息越多，错误越多。

因此按下面优先级纠错：

1. **主干先正确**：主语 + 谓语不能错；
2. **时态 / 人称先稳定**；
3. **再检查介词、冠词、代词**；
4. **最后才优化连接和表达丰富度**。

例如孩子说：

> She usually play badminton after school with her friends because she like it.

不要一次指出五个问题。

优先抓主干：

> She usually **plays** ... because she **likes** it.

重新说完整句，再继续扩展。

---

# 11. 达标标准

Welcome Unit 阶段，不要求使用复杂从句。

一个基础句如果可以做到以下任意一种，就算扩句训练达标：

- 增加 2–3 个有效信息成分；
- 用 `and / but / because / so` 自然连接；
- 从 1 个短句扩展为 2–3 句连贯表达；
- 不依赖中文逐字翻译；
- 扩展后主干语法仍然正确。

核心目标：

> **先把简单句说正确，再把信息说丰富；不是为了“长”而制造复杂句。**
