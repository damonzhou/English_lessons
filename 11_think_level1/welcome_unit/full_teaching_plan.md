# Think Level 1 — Welcome Unit 完整家长备课稿（单文件可直接上课版）

## 1. 课程定位

Welcome Unit 不按普通单元慢速讲解，而作为进入 Level 1 前的 A1 基础诊断和口语自动化单元。

当前课程目标：
- 快速确认 A1 基础是否存在真实知识缺口；
- 把“认识 / 会做题”的基础句型训练到“听到能反应、看到提示能迅速说、真实情境能主动调用”；
- 建立之后所有 Think Unit 都沿用的听说训练流程。

课堂核心流程：

**Listening Input → Imitate → Pattern Drill → Sentence Expansion → Supported Speaking → Real-use Task → Delayed Recall**

建议 4–6 次课，每次 20–30 分钟。

### 课堂时间优先级

正常情况下，课堂中约 70%–80% 的有效训练时间用于：
- 听教材音频 / 听家长问题或指令；
- 跟读与语音模仿；
- 核心句型替换；
- 问答；
- 扩句；
- 角色扮演；
- 半开放 / 自由表达。

书面练习主要用于：
- 快速确认是否理解；
- 记录真实错误；
- 课后巩固。

不要把课堂主要时间花在抄写、长篇语法讲解或机械选择题上。

---

# 使用本备课稿的方法

每节课已经直接放入“当堂精选素材”，家长上课时原则上只需打开本文件。

每个核心句型按以下顺序使用：

1. **Model**：家长先说，孩子听和跟读；
2. **Core Drill Set**：当堂 6–10 个精选替换；
3. **Variation Drill**：主语 / 肯否 / 问句 / 双槽变化；
4. **Listening Prompt**：孩子不看文字，只听并反应；
5. **Sentence Expansion**：从短句增加有效信息；
6. **Real-use Task**：撤掉模板进入真实表达；
7. **Delayed Recall**：1–7 天后再抽查。

完整备用素材仍保留在：
- `listening_speaking_drill_bank.md`
- `substitution_drill_bank.md`
- `sentence_expansion_templates.md`

这些文件作为“随机池”，不需要每节课全部使用。

# Lesson W1 — All About Me

## 1. Exit Goal

孩子能够：
- 听懂姓名、年龄、来自哪里、住在哪里等基础个人信息；
- 2–3 秒内开始回答常见个人信息问题；
- 连续用 4–6 句介绍自己；
- 简单介绍一位朋友或家人。

## 2. Core Patterns

1. `My name's ______. / I'm ______.`
2. `I'm ______ years old.`
3. `I'm from ______.`
4. `I live in ______.`
5. `This is my ______.`
6. `His/Her name is ______.`
7. `He/She is ______ years old.`
8. `He/She is from ______.`

## 3. Listening Warm-up

家长随机提问，不展示文字：
- What's your name?
- How old are you?
- Where are you from?
- Where do you live?
- Who's your best friend?
- How old is your best friend?
- Where is your best friend from?

第一轮只观察启动速度，不急于纠错。

第二轮要求尽量完整回答：

> I'm twelve years old.

而不是只回答：

> Twelve.

---

## 4. Core Pattern 1 — `I'm from ______.`

### Model

> I'm from Wuhan.

### Core Drill Set — 单槽替换

家长只说地点，孩子每次必须说完整句：

1. Wuhan
2. Beijing
3. Shanghai
4. Chengdu
5. Xi'an
6. Guangzhou
7. London
8. New York
9. Sydney
10. Tokyo

示例：

Parent: `Beijing.`

Child: `I'm from Beijing.`

### Variation Drill — 主语变化

家长给：
- she / Wuhan
- he / Beijing
- they / Shanghai
- we / China
- my friend / Wuhan
- my parents / China
- Lucy / London
- Tom and Jack / Sydney

孩子说：
- `She's from Wuhan.`
- `He's from Beijing.`
- `They're from Shanghai.`
- `We're from China.`
- `My friend is from Wuhan.`
- `My parents are from China.`
- `Lucy is from London.`
- `Tom and Jack are from Sydney.`

### Listening Prompt

家长只说完整句，孩子先判断人物，再复述：

- `She's from Wuhan.`
- `They're from Beijing.`
- `We're from China.`
- `Tom is from Shanghai.`
- `My parents are from Chengdu.`

### Question Drill

家长随机问：
- Are you from Wuhan?
- Are you from Beijing?
- Is Lucy from Shanghai?
- Are your parents from Wuhan?

要求完整短答：

> Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

---

## 5. Core Pattern 2 — `I'm ______ years old.`

### Model

> I'm twelve years old.

### Core Drill Set

1. eleven
2. twelve
3. thirteen
4. fourteen
5. fifteen
6. ten

### Person Change

- Lucy / twelve
- Tom / thirteen
- my brother / fourteen
- my sister / eleven
- my friends / twelve

孩子：
- `Lucy is twelve years old.`
- `Tom is thirteen years old.`
- `My brother is fourteen years old.`
- `My sister is eleven years old.`
- `My friends are twelve years old.`

### Listening Prompt

家长说：

> Lucy is thirteen years old.

孩子回答：

> How old is Lucy? — She's thirteen years old.

随机人物：Lucy / Tom / Emma / Jack / Amy / David。

---

## 6. Core Pattern 3 — Introduce Someone

### Model

> This is my friend Lucy. Her name is Lucy. She's twelve years old. She's from Wuhan.

### Core Multi-slot Set

家长每次只读四个提示：

1. friend / Lucy / 12 / Wuhan
2. cousin / Tom / 13 / Beijing
3. classmate / Emma / 12 / Shanghai
4. brother / Jack / 14 / Chengdu
5. sister / Amy / 11 / Xi'an
6. friend / David / 13 / London

孩子连续说 3–4 句。

### Listening Information Gap

家长口头说：

> This is Emma. She's twelve years old. She's from Shanghai. She lives in Wuhan now.

孩子不看文字，只记录：

> Emma / 12 / Shanghai / Wuhan

然后根据关键词复述。

再换 Tom / Lucy / Amy。

---

## 7. Sentence Expansion

基础：

> I'm from Wuhan.

扩展：

> I'm from Wuhan, China.

再扩：

> I'm from Wuhan, China, and I live there with my family.

另一个基础句：

> My friend is from Wuhan.

扩展：

> My best friend is from Wuhan.

再扩：

> My best friend is from Wuhan, and we're in the same school.

家长追问：
- Where exactly?
- Who do you live with?
- Is your friend in your class?

孩子只增加 1–2 个自然信息即可。

---

## 8. Supported Speaking

### 高支架

> Hi, I'm ______.
> I'm ______ years old.
> I'm from ______.
> I live in ______.
> My best friend is ______.

### 中支架

只给：

> name → age → from → live → friend

### 低支架

家长只说：

> Tell me about yourself.

---

## 9. Real-use Task

情景：第一次见一个新同学。

要求：
- 自我介绍 30–45 秒；
- 主动问对方 2–3 个问题；
- 最后用 2–3 句介绍一个朋友。

## 10. Delayed Recall

1–2 天后只给关键词：

> name / age / Wuhan / friend

3–4 天后只问：

> Tell me about yourself and one friend.

更多备用素材见 `substitution_drill_bank.md` 的个人信息部分。

# Lesson W2 — Classroom Survival English

## 1. Exit Goal

遇到没听清、不会说、不知道意思、不知道拼写或需要帮助时，能够不用中文求助。

## 2. Core Patterns

1. `Excuse me.`
2. `Can you say that again, please?`
3. `Sorry, I don't understand.`
4. `What does ______ mean?`
5. `How do you say ______ in English?`
6. `How do you spell ______?`
7. `Can you help me, please?`
8. `Can I ask a question, please?`

---

## 3. Core Pattern 1 — 没听清

### Model

> Can you say that again, please?

### Listening Situation Set

家长故意：
1. 说一句很快的简单话；
2. 背对孩子说一句；
3. 故意压低声音；
4. 连续给两个指令；
5. 说一个孩子没捕捉到的时间。

孩子一旦没听清，必须自己说：

> Can you say that again, please?

### Upgrade

可选升级：

> Sorry, I didn't catch that. Can you say it again, please?

Welcome 阶段不要求必须掌握升级句。

---

## 4. Core Pattern 2 — `What does ______ mean?`

### Core Drill Set

家长只给词：

1. helmet
2. shelf
3. folder
4. glue
5. drawer
6. schedule
7. subject
8. message

孩子每次完整问：

> What does "helmet" mean?

### Listening Prompt

家长把词放进简短句：

> Put the book on the shelf.

孩子不知道 `shelf` 时，不允许家长主动翻译；要求孩子自己发起：

> What does "shelf" mean?

---

## 5. Core Pattern 3 — `How do you say ______ in English?`

### Core Drill Set

可用实物或中文概念：

1. 文件夹
2. 订书机
3. 胶棒
4. 抽屉
5. 尺子
6. 笔袋
7. 书架
8. 橡皮

孩子每次完整问句。

### Real-object Drill

家长直接指一个真实物品，不说英文。

如果孩子不知道，必须使用目标句型。

---

## 6. Core Pattern 4 — `How do you spell ______?`

### Core Drill Set

1. calendar
2. dictionary
3. notebook
4. ruler
5. pencil case
6. classroom
7. sandwich
8. badminton

家长先口头告诉单词，孩子主动问拼写。

之后交换角色，让孩子说一个词，家长问拼写。

---

## 7. Rapid Response Drill

家长只给情境，不给英文：

- 没听清
- 不知道意思
- 不知道英文怎么说
- 不知道拼写
- 需要帮助
- 想问问题

孩子必须 2–3 秒内说对应表达。

第二轮家长随机打乱顺序。

第三轮家长直接制造真实情境，不再说情境名称。

---

## 8. Listening Repair Drill

家长连续说 6–8 个短任务，其中故意制造问题。

例如：

> Put your notebook next to the folder.

如果孩子不知道 folder，目标不是猜，而是主动修复沟通。

课堂目标：让孩子形成习惯：

**没听懂 → 用英语修复 → 继续任务**。

---

## 9. Real-use Task

最后 5 分钟规定：学习过程中不能用中文求助。

家长至少制造：
- 1 次没听清；
- 1 个生词；
- 1 个不知道拼写的词；
- 1 个需要帮助的任务。

## 10. Delayed Recall

第二天学习其他内容时故意说快一次，观察孩子是否会主动说：

> Can you say that again, please?

以后所有 Think 课程都继续使用这些 classroom survival expressions。

# Lesson W3 — What's That? / Where Is It?

## 1. Exit Goal

孩子能够：
- 听懂位置指令；
- 描述真实物品位置；
- 正确使用 `this / that / these / those`；
- 在简单句中正确使用 `him / her / it / them`。

## 2. Core Patterns

1. `What's this/that?`
2. `What are these/those?`
3. `The ______ is on/under/next to/behind/in front of ______.`
4. `Where is the ______?`
5. `It's ______ the ______.`
6. `I like him/her/it/them.`

## 3. 当堂物品池

准备真实物品优先：

- pen
- pencil
- notebook
- book
- phone
- tablet
- ruler
- pencil case
- schoolbag
- water bottle
- eraser
- keys
- laptop

位置：

- on
- under
- next to
- behind
- in front of
- between
- in

---

## 4. Core Pattern 1 — `The ______ is ______ the ______.`

### Model

> The pen is on the book.

### Single-slot Drill — 只换方位

固定 pen / book：

1. on
2. under
3. next to
4. behind
5. in front of

孩子每次说完整句。

### Single-slot Drill — 只换物品

固定 `on the desk`：

1. phone
2. notebook
3. ruler
4. keys
5. pencil case
6. book
7. tablet

### Multi-slot Drill

家长口头给：

1. phone / under / chair
2. notebook / on / desk
3. keys / next to / laptop
4. schoolbag / behind / chair
5. pencil / between / books
6. ruler / in front of / notebook
7. eraser / in / pencil case
8. phone / on / book

孩子生成完整句。

---

## 5. Listening Command Drill

孩子不看文字，只听并执行。

### 一步指令

1. `Put the pen under the book.`
2. `Put the phone next to the laptop.`
3. `Put the ruler behind the notebook.`
4. `Put the keys in front of the bag.`
5. `Put the eraser in the pencil case.`
6. `Put the phone on the book.`

### 两步指令

1. `Put the pen under the book, then put the ruler next to the pen.`
2. `Put the phone on the desk, then put the keys behind the phone.`
3. `Put the eraser in the pencil case, then put the pencil case under the chair.`

完成后交换角色，孩子给家长下指令。

---

## 6. Core Pattern 2 — `this / that / these / those`

### 真物 Drill

家长只改变“距离 + 数量”：

- 近处单数 → `What's this?`
- 远处单数 → `What's that?`
- 近处复数 → `What are these?`
- 远处复数 → `What are those?`

### Core Object Set

1. pen
2. notebook
3. ruler
4. keys
5. books
6. pencils
7. shoes
8. headphones

要求完整回答：

> It's a ruler.

> They're books.

而不是只说：

> ruler / books

---

## 7. Core Pattern 3 — Object Pronouns

### Model

> I like Tom. → I like him.

### Core Drill Set

家长说名词，孩子把整句改成代词：

1. Dad → him
2. Mum → her
3. Lucy → her
4. Jack → him
5. the book → it
6. the phone → it
7. the shoes → them
8. my friends → them
9. my parents → them
10. my sister and me → us

### Sentence Set

- `I can see Tom.` → `I can see him.`
- `Please call Lucy.` → `Please call her.`
- `I like this book.` → `I like it.`
- `I can't find my keys.` → `I can't find them.`
- `Please help my sister and me.` → `Please help us.`

---

## 8. Listening Game — Describe What Changed

1. 摆好 4–5 件物品；
2. 孩子看 10 秒；
3. 孩子闭眼；
4. 家长移动 1–2 件物品；
5. 孩子睁眼后用完整句描述变化。

例如：

> The phone is under the book now.

> The ruler is next to the laptop now.

---

## 9. Sentence Expansion

基础：

> The book is on the desk.

扩展：

> The English book is on the desk.

再扩：

> The English book is on the desk next to my laptop.

如果信息过多，拆成：

> My English book is on the desk. It's next to my laptop.

---

## 10. Real-use Task

不看书描述自己的书桌或房间：
- 4–6 个物品；
- 至少 3 种方位介词；
- 连续 4–6 句；
- 最后给家长 2 个摆放指令。

## 11. Delayed Recall

隔 2–3 天突然问：

> Where are your keys?

> What's that?

> Where's your phone?

看是否能不回忆规则直接回答。

更多位置素材见 `listening_speaking_drill_bank.md`。

## 5. Listening Time Drill

家长口头随机说：

1. half past six
2. quarter to seven
3. seven o'clock
4. quarter past seven
5. twenty past seven
6. ten to eight
7. half past eight
8. quarter past nine
9. ten o'clock

第一轮：孩子只说数字时间。

第二轮：孩子把时间放进句子：

> I get up at half past six.

### Reverse Drill

家长给数字：

> 7:30

孩子说：

> half past seven

---

## 6. Core Pattern 1 — `I ______ at ______.`

### Core Drill Set

家长口头给：

1. get up / 6:30
2. have breakfast / 7:00
3. go to school / 7:30
4. have lunch / 12:00
5. get home / 5:00
6. do homework / 7:00
7. read books / 8:30
8. go to bed / 10:00

孩子每次说完整句。

### Question Variation

家长问：
- What time do you get up?
- What time do you have breakfast?
- What time do you go to school?
- What time do you get home?
- What time do you go to bed?

第一轮按真实信息回答。

第二轮家长随机打乱问题顺序。

---

## 7. Core Pattern 2 — `I usually ______ after school.`

### Action Core Drill Set

1. do my homework
2. read books
3. play badminton
4. play basketball
5. listen to music
6. practice the piano
7. watch videos
8. go swimming
9. talk with my friends
10. have a snack

孩子每次完整说：

> I usually read books after school.

### Frequency Variation

频率槽：
- always
- usually
- often
- sometimes
- rarely
- never

组合：

1. usually / play badminton
2. often / read books
3. sometimes / watch videos
4. never / play computer games
5. often / practice the piano
6. sometimes / go swimming

孩子要根据频率变化生成完整句。

### Person Change

1. she / badminton
2. Tom / read books
3. my sister / piano
4. they / basketball
5. my friend / music

孩子：

> She usually plays badminton after school.

> Tom usually reads books after school.

> They usually play basketball after school.

这里重点观察第三人称单数是否自动变化。

---

## 8. Listening Mini-interview

家长不展示问题，逐个口头问：

- What time do you get up?
- What do you do before school?
- What do you usually do after school?
- How often do you play sports?
- When do you do your homework?
- What do you do in the evening?
- What time do you go to bed?

第一轮逐问逐答。

第二轮只说：

> Tell me about your school day.

---

## 9. Sentence Expansion

### Expansion 1

基础：

> I play badminton.

加频率：

> I usually play badminton.

加时间：

> I usually play badminton after school.

加人物：

> I usually play badminton with my friends after school.

加原因：

> I usually play badminton with my friends after school because it's fun.

如果太长：

> I usually play badminton with my friends after school. We often play for about an hour because it's fun.

### Expansion 2 — `I read books.`

家长依次问：
- When?
- Where?
- How often?
- Why?

可形成：

> I usually read books in my room before bed because it's relaxing.

不要求每次加入所有信息。

---

## 10. Real-use Task — My School Day

只给关键词：

> get up → breakfast → school → lunch → home → homework → dinner → bed

要求：
- 连续 6–8 句；
- 至少 4 个时间点；
- 至少 1 个频率副词；
- 至少 1 个扩展信息；
- 尽量使用 `then / after that / in the evening` 中至少一个。

## 11. Delayed Recall

Day 2：随机给 5 个“动作 + 时间”。

Day 4：只问 `What do you usually do after school?`

Day 7：再做一次 `Tell me about your weekday.`

更多 daily routine 素材见 `listening_speaking_drill_bank.md`。

## 4. Core Pattern 1 — Positive / Negative

### Model

### Core Drill Set

1. phone / yes
2. tablet / no
3. bike / yes
4. smartwatch / no
5. guitar / yes
6. hamster / no
7. laptop / yes
8. skateboard / no
9. camera / yes
10. dictionary / yes

孩子每次说完整句。

---

## 5. Core Pattern 2 — Question Drill

孩子问家长：

> Have you got a bike?

当堂问答素材：

1. phone
2. laptop
3. bike
4. smartwatch
5. headphones
6. camera
7. guitar
8. tablet

家长随机回答 yes/no。

第二轮交换角色。

第三轮不展示物品列表，孩子自己想 3 个问题。

---

## 6. Core Pattern 3 — Person Change

家长口头提示：

1. Dad / laptop / yes
2. Mum / smartwatch / no
3. Lucy / bike / no
4. Tom / skateboard / yes
5. my parents / car / yes
6. my friend / cat / yes
7. Emma / tablet / question
8. Jack / camera / question

孩子生成：

> Dad has got a laptop.

> Mum hasn't got a smartwatch.

> Has Emma got a tablet?

> Have my parents got a car?

重点：

**I / you / we / they → have**

**he / she / person name → has**

---

## 7. Listening Information Gap

家长只说一次或两次：

> Tom has got a bike, a phone and a basketball, but he hasn't got a tablet or a skateboard.

孩子只记录关键词：

> Tom: bike / phone / basketball / no tablet / no skateboard

然后复述。

第二组：

> Lucy has got a tablet, headphones and a camera, but she hasn't got a bike.

第三组家长自己随机组合 3–5 件物品。

---

## 8. Sentence Expansion

基础：

> I've got a bike.

扩展：

> I've got a new bike.

再扩：

> I've got a new bike at home.

再扩：

> I've got a new bike at home, and I really like it.

最后：

> I've got a new bike at home, and I often ride it with my dad on weekends.

不要要求每次都扩到最后一级。

---

## 9. Real-use Task — Things I've Got

要求：
- 说 3 件自己有的东西；
- 说 2 件自己没有的东西；
- 问家长 3 个 `Have you got ...?` 问题；
- 最后用 2–3 句总结一名家人的情况。

## 10. Delayed Recall

Day 2：

> Dad / laptop

> Lucy / bike / no

> Tom / phone / question

Day 4：

> Tell me three things you've got and two things you haven't got.

Day 7：随机问一个家人：

> Has Mum got ...?

更多物品槽见 `substitution_drill_bank.md`。

# Lesson W6 — `I like` vs `I'd like`

## 1. Exit Goal

孩子能够：
- 通过情境判断“一般喜好”与“当前请求”；
- 自动切换 `I like ...` / `I'd like ...`；
- 听懂基础点餐问题；
- 完成 45–60 秒简单点餐对话。

## 2. Core Patterns

1. `I like ______.`
2. `I really like ______ because ______.`
3. `I'd like ______, please.`
4. `What would you like?`
5. `Would you like ______?`
6. `Yes, please. / No, thank you.`
7. `Anything else?`

## 3. Food / Drink 当堂素材池

### Food
- pizza
- noodles
- rice
- chicken
- fish
- sandwich
- hamburger
- salad
- soup
- dumplings

### Fruit
- apple
- orange
- banana
- grapes
- watermelon

### Drinks
- water
- milk
- orange juice
- apple juice
- tea
- hot chocolate

---

## 4. Core Pattern 1 — `I like ...`

### Core Drill Set

家长只说项目：

1. pizza
2. noodles
3. chicken
4. dumplings
5. apples
6. orange juice
7. badminton
8. music
9. reading
10. swimming

孩子：

> I like pizza.

第二轮升级：

> I really like pizza.

第三轮任选 3 个加原因：

> I really like pizza because it's delicious.

---

## 5. Core Pattern 2 — `I'd like ..., please.`

### Ordering Drill

家长说：

> You're at a café. Pizza.

孩子：

> I'd like a pizza, please.

当堂素材：

1. sandwich
2. pizza
3. soup
4. orange juice
5. water
6. apple
7. salad
8. hot chocolate

注意冠词 / some 根据表达自然处理，不作为本课主要讲解点。

---

## 6. Rapid Contrast Drill — Like vs Would Like

家长只说情境：

1. You like noodles.
2. You're ordering noodles now.
3. You like pizza.
4. You're ordering pizza now.
5. You like orange juice.
6. You're asking for orange juice now.
7. You like apples.
8. You're choosing an apple now.

孩子必须自己切换：

> I like noodles.

> I'd like some noodles, please.

目标是“听情境 → 直接反应”，而不是先在脑中背规则。

---

## 7. Listening Role-play — Parent as Server

家长不展示文字，依次问：

- What would you like to eat?
- What would you like to drink?
- Would you like some fruit?
- Would you like an apple or an orange?
- Anything else?
- Is that all?

孩子要求尽量完整句回答。

第二轮交换角色，孩子当服务员。

### 第二轮可增加追问

- Would you like some soup?
- Would you like water or juice?
- What fruit would you like?

---

## 8. Sentence Expansion

基础：

> I like pizza.

扩展：

> I really like pizza.

加原因：

> I really like pizza because it's delicious.

加频率 / 时间：

> I often eat pizza on weekends because it's delicious.

再做一个：

> I like swimming.

家长追问：
- When?
- Who with?
- Why?

可能得到：

> I often go swimming with my dad on weekends because it's fun.

---

## 9. Real-use Task — Café / Picnic Role-play

完成 45–60 秒对话。

孩子至少需要：
- 点 3–4 样东西；
- 回答 2 个 `Would you like ...?`；
- 自己提出至少 1 个问题；
- 至少一次正确使用 `I'd like ...`；
- 最后再用 `I like ...` 表达一种平时喜好。

## 10. Delayed Recall

Day 2：快速做 6 组“喜欢 / 现在想要”情境切换。

Day 4：直接做 café role-play，不先复习规则。

Day 7：随机问：

> What food do you like?

然后立刻切换：

> You're at a café now. What would you like?

更多餐饮素材见 `listening_speaking_drill_bank.md`。

# 2. 每节课固定听说训练结构

## A. Listening Input（3–5 分钟）

优先使用教材 Audio / Video；没有对应音频时，家长口头提供简短指令、问题或信息。

原则：
- 第一遍不看文字；
- 先抓主旨 / 任务；
- 第二遍抓细节；
- 对照后再听确认。

## B. Imitation（2–3 分钟）

选 2–4 个核心句跟读：
- 注意重音；
- 连读；
- 语调；
- 不逐词念。

## C. Pattern Drill（5–8 分钟）

每个核心句型：
- 跟读 2–3 次；
- 单槽替换 6–10 次；
- 多槽替换 3–5 次；
- 必要时做主语 / 肯否 / 疑问变化。

优先使用本备课稿中已经列出的“Core Drill Set”。

如果当天仍需要更多素材，再从大素材库随机抽取。

## D. Sentence Expansion（2–4 分钟）

在主干稳定后加入：
- when
- where
- who with
- how often
- why
- how long

目标不是制造超长句，而是增加有效信息。

## E. Supported Speaking（3–5 分钟）

按以下顺序撤支架：

1. 完整句型；
2. 替换词；
3. 关键词；
4. 问题；
5. 场景；
6. 自由表达。

## F. Exit Task（2–4 分钟）

最后必须有真实小任务，不能以“教材题做完”作为结束标准。

---

# 3. 一节课如何控制训练量

20–30 分钟课的建议目标：

- 教材音频 / 家长口头输入：5–10 个可理解小片段或指令；
- 跟读：3–5 个关键句；
- 真正进入高频 Drill 的核心句型：2–4 个；
- 每个重点核心句型：10–18 个不同完整句输出；
- 问答 / 角色扮演：至少 5–10 个回合；
- Sentence Expansion：至少 1–2 个基础句；
- Exit Task：1 次完整真实表达。

如果准确度明显下降：

**减少新内容，不增加堆叠训练量。**

---

# 4. 分散重复计划

每个核心句型：

### Day 1
- 10–18 个不同完整句输出；
- 1 次真实使用。

### Day 2
- 3–5 次快速复现；
- 尽量不看完整句型。

### Day 3–4
- 换一批素材做 5–8 次；
- 只给关键词或问题。

### Day 7
- 放到新的口语、听力反应或 Writing 情境中再次调用。

达到以下大部分标准后停止高频 Drill：
- 2–3 秒内能开始；
- 随机换槽仍准确；
- 主语变化时动词能自动调整；
- 不依赖完整模板；
- 一周后仍能主动调用。

---

# 5. 家长纠错规则

## 偶发错误

直接 recast + 重说一次：

Child:

> She play basketball.

Parent:

> She plays basketball.

Child:

> She plays basketball.

然后继续，不展开语法讲座。

## 连续错误

如果连续出现：
- `She play ...`
- `My dad go ...`
- `Lucy like ...`

暂停扩句，回到主干：

> I play → She plays → They play → He plays

稳定后再恢复长句。

## 跨多次仍然错误

记录到 `07_grammar/grammar_progress.md`，必要时触发专项微课。

# 7. 最终判断标准

Welcome Unit 不以书面正确率作为唯一标准。

重点记录：

1. **Listening reaction**：听到熟悉指令 / 问题能否迅速反应；
2. **启动速度**：熟悉问题是否 2–3 秒内开始回答；
3. **句型准确度**：基础主干是否稳定；
4. **替换能力**：换主语、动作、时间后能否保持正确；
5. **扩句能力**：能否自然增加时间、地点、人物、原因；
6. **连续表达**：能否从单句过渡到 3–6 句；
7. **主动调用**：是否无需提醒句型名称就能在真实情境使用。

如果书面基础正确、Pattern Drill 在提示下稳定，但真实表达仍慢，说明重点仍然是“自动化 + 延迟提取”，不需要因此完整回退 Starter。
