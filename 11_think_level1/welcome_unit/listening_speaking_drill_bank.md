# Welcome Unit 听力与口语训练素材库

> 目标：课堂尽量把时间用于“听到 → 反应 → 说完整句 → 变化 → 扩展 → 真实使用”。
>
> 本文件优先服务口语和听力，不作为书面题库。

---

# A. All About Me

## A1. Listening prompts

家长随机提问：
- What's your name?
- How old are you?
- Where are you from?
- Where do you live?
- Who's your best friend?
- Is your best friend from Wuhan?
- Are you twelve years old?

训练规则：
- 不展示文字；
- 2–3 秒内开始回答；
- 第一轮允许短答，第二轮要求完整句。

## A2. Rapid response bank

提示 → 完整句：
- Wuhan → `I'm from Wuhan.`
- twelve → `I'm twelve years old.`
- China → `I'm from China.`
- my friend / Lucy → `This is my friend Lucy.`
- Lucy / thirteen → `She's thirteen years old.`
- Tom / Beijing → `He's from Beijing.`

## A3. Multi-slot cards

随机组合：
- friend / Lucy / 12 / Wuhan
- cousin / Tom / 13 / Beijing
- classmate / Emma / 12 / Shanghai
- brother / Jack / 14 / Chengdu
- sister / Amy / 11 / Xi'an

要求连续说 3–4 句。

# B. Classroom Survival English

## B1. Situation → English

家长只给情境，不给英语：
- 没听清
- 不知道单词意思
- 不知道英文怎么说
- 不知道怎么拼
- 需要帮助
- 想问问题

目标表达：
- `Can you say that again, please?`
- `What does ... mean?`
- `How do you say ... in English?`
- `How do you spell ...?`
- `Can you help me, please?`
- `Can I ask a question, please?`

## B2. Listening repair drill

家长故意：
- 说得稍快；
- 换一个陌生词；
- 把一个词拼出来；
- 给一个孩子没听懂的短指令。

孩子必须先用课堂英语处理问题，再继续任务。

# C. Objects and Positions

## C1. Listening command bank

只听并执行：
- Put the pen on the book.
- Put the ruler under the notebook.
- Put the phone next to the laptop.
- Put the keys behind the bag.
- Put the pencil case in front of the book.
- Put the eraser between the two books.
- Move the phone from the desk to the chair.
- Put two pencils next to the notebook.

升级：连续给两步指令。

例如：
`Put the pen under the book, then put the ruler next to the pen.`

## C2. Describe what changed

家长摆放物品，孩子闭眼；家长移动 1–2 件物品。

孩子睁眼后说：
- `The phone is under the book now.`
- `The ruler is next to the laptop.`

## C3. Question bank

- Where's the pen?
- Where are the keys?
- What's this?
- What's that?
- What are these?
- What are those?
- Can you see the ruler?
- Is the phone under the book?

# I. 每次课听说量建议

一节 20–30 分钟的课，目标不是“讲多少”，而是尽量产生足够多的有效听说回合。

建议：
- 教材音频/家长口头输入：5–10 个可理解小片段或指令；
- 跟读：3–5 个关键句；
- 核心句型：2–4 个；
- 每个核心句型完整口头输出：10–18 次变式；
- 问答/角色扮演：至少 5–10 个回合；
- Exit Task：1 次完整真实表达。

如果准确度明显下降，优先减少新内容，而不是继续堆训练量。
