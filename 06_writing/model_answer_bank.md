# Think Level 1 写作原创范文库

## 使用原则

这些范文是围绕 Think Level 1 主题自行编写的 A2 级示例，不复制教材原文。

正确使用顺序：
1. 先自己列提纲；
2. 尽量先写第一稿；
3. 再看范文；
4. 比较内容、顺序、连接词和表达；
5. 修改自己的文章；
6. 只摘取 2–3 个真正能迁移的表达。

不要整篇背诵。

# Unit 1 — Routine

## Model｜My School Day

I usually get up at 6:45 on school days. After breakfast, I go to school by car with my dad. Classes start at eight o’clock. I often have lunch at school with my classmates. After school, I do my homework first and then I read or play badminton. In the evening, I have dinner with my family. I like my school days because I can learn new things and spend time with my friends.

可迁移表达：
- on school days
- do my homework first and then ...
- spend time with ...

# Unit 2 — Email / Present continuous

## Model｜An Email from the Shopping Centre

Hi Amy,

I’m at the shopping centre with my mum now. She’s looking at some shoes, and I’m waiting outside the shop. A lot of people are buying clothes because there’s a big sale today. I’m not buying anything at the moment, but I’m looking for a birthday present for my friend. After that, we’re going to have lunch here.

See you tomorrow!

Lucy

可迁移表达：
- at the moment
- look for ...
- After that, ...

# Unit 3 — Food

## Model｜What I Eat in a Day

I usually have bread, an egg and some milk for breakfast. At school, I have rice, vegetables and meat for lunch. I sometimes eat fruit in the afternoon, but I don’t eat many sweets. For dinner, my family often has noodles or rice with vegetables. I drink a lot of water every day. I think my diet is quite healthy, but I should eat more fruit because it is good for me.

可迁移表达：
- a lot of
- quite healthy
- be good for ...

# Unit 4 — Invitation

## Model｜Birthday Invitation

Hi Emma,

I’m having a birthday party this Saturday, and I’d love you to come. It starts at 3 p.m. at my home. We’re going to play games, listen to music and have a big birthday cake. You don’t need to bring any food, but please bring your favourite board game if you can. My dad can take you home after the party. Please tell me if you can come.

See you!

Mia

可迁移表达：
- I’d love you to come.
- You don’t need to ...
- Please tell me if ...

# Unit 5 — Home / Blog

## Model｜My Favourite Room

My favourite room is my bedroom. It isn’t very big, but it is bright and comfortable. There is a desk next to the window, and I usually do my homework there. I also have a small bookshelf with my favourite books. Last month, I put some photos on the wall, and now the room feels more personal. I like spending time there because it is quiet and I can relax after a busy day.

可迁移表达：
- next to ...
- feel more personal
- relax after a busy day

# Unit 6 — Thank-you note

## Model｜Thank You for Helping Me

Dear Lily,

Thank you for helping me with my maths homework yesterday. I didn’t understand one of the problems, and I was starting to feel worried. You explained it slowly and showed me another example. After that, I could finish the exercise by myself. Your help made me feel much better. I’m very lucky to have a friend like you.

Thanks again!

Anna

可迁移表达：
- help me with ...
- by myself
- make me feel ...

# Unit 7 — Housework

## Model｜Housework I Do at Home

Everyone in my family does some housework. I have to tidy my room every day, and I usually take out the rubbish after dinner. At weekends, I sometimes help my mum wash the dishes. I don’t have to cook because my parents usually do that. I don’t really like cleaning, but I think teenagers should help at home. It makes life easier for everyone, and it also teaches us to be responsible.

可迁移表达：
- take out the rubbish
- help at home
- make life easier for ...

# Unit 8 — Sporting event

## Model｜Our School Sports Day

Last Friday, our school had a sports day on the playground. Many students took part in running, jumping and ball games. I joined the 800-metre race. While I was waiting for the race to start, I felt very nervous. At first, I ran too fast, but then I slowed down and kept going. I didn’t win, but I finished the race. I was tired and proud because I didn’t give up.

可迁移表达：
- take part in ...
- At first, ... but then ...
- give up

# Unit 9 — Weather event

## Model｜An Amazing Rainstorm

Hi Ben,

We had a huge rainstorm yesterday afternoon. The sky became very dark, and the wind was much stronger than usual. I was on my way home when the rain started. Luckily, my dad came to pick me up. Some streets had a lot of water, so the drive home took longer than normal. It was one of the heaviest rainstorms I can remember. I was a little scared, but we got home safely.

Take care,

Leo

可迁移表达：
- stronger than usual
- on my way home
- get home safely

# Unit 10 — Informal email / Plans

## Model｜A Day Out in My City

Hi Jack,

I’m really happy that you’re coming to visit next Saturday. In the morning, we’re going to walk around East Lake because the view is beautiful. Then we can have lunch near the lake. In the afternoon, I’d like to take you to a science museum. It’s interesting, and there are lots of things to try. Let’s meet at my home at 9 a.m. What do you think of the plan?

See you soon!

David

可迁移表达：
- I’m really happy that ...
- I’d like to ...
- What do you think of ...?

# Unit 11 — Phone message

## Model｜A Phone Message

Mum,

Mrs Green called at 4:20. She said the English club meeting tomorrow will start at 4:30 instead of 4:00. It will be in Room 302, not the library. She asked me to bring my workbook and a pen. She also wants you to call her this evening if I can’t go.

— Amy

可迁移表达：
- instead of ...
- She asked me to ...
- if I can’t ...

说明：phone message 的重点是信息准确，不需要为了达到 60–80 词故意写长。

# Unit 12 — Someone I admire

## Model｜Someone I Admire

The person I admire most is my grandfather. He is kind, patient and always ready to help other people. When he was young, he worked very hard and learned many useful skills by himself. Even now, he still enjoys reading and learning new things. When I have a problem, he listens carefully and gives me useful advice. I admire him because he never stops learning. He has taught me to stay curious and work hard.

可迁移表达：
- be ready to help ...
- give me useful advice
- never stop doing ...

# 范文升级规则

当 A2 标准范文已经明显偏简单时，不直接换成大量高级句型，而是在原结构上增加：

1. 一个更具体的例子；
2. 一个原因或结果；
3. 一个感受；
4. 一个自然的时间/逻辑连接；
5. 1–2 个已经学过且能够控制的稍丰富表达。

例如：

基础：
> I like swimming because it is fun.

升级：
> I like swimming because it helps me relax after a busy school day.

升级的标准是“信息更准确、表达更自然”，不是“句子越长越好”。
